# Citing KaMPIng-v2

KaMPIng-v2 and its core interface are described in

> T. N. Uhl, M. Schimek, D. Brommer. **Concepts in Practice: C++ MPI Bindings for the HPC Ecosystem — From a
> Standardizable Core to a Composable Interface.** In *Recent Advances in the Message Passing Interface (EuroMPI
> 2026)*, Lecture Notes in Computer Science, Springer.
> [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-40777-1_2](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-40777-1_2) ·
> preprint: [arXiv:2606.09102](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09102)

If you use KaMPIng-v2 in your work, please cite it:

```bibtex
@inproceedings{UhlSB26,
  author        = {Tim Niklas Uhl and Matthias Schimek and Daniel Brommer},

}
```

The paper realizes the design principles of

> C. N. Avans, A. A. Correa, S. Ghosh, M. Schimek, J. Schuchart, A. Skjellum, E. D. Suggs, T. N. Uhl. **Concepts for
> Designing Modern C++ Interfaces for MPI.** In *EuroMPI 2025*, Lecture Notes in Computer Science, pp. 165–183,
> Springer. [doi:10.1007/978-3-032-07194-1_10](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-07194-1_10) ·
> preprint: [arXiv:2506.14610](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.14610)

KaMPIng-v2 is the successor of [KaMPIng](https://github.com/kamping-site/kamping). It is developed at the
[Algorithm Engineering Group](https://ae.iti.kit.edu) at Karlsruhe Institute of Technology and released under the
[Boost Software License 1.0](https://github.com/kamping-site/kamping-v2/blob/main/LICENSE).
